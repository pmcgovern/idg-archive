About this archive

IDG — International Data Group — was founded by Patrick J. McGovern in 1964 and grew into the world's largest technology media company, publishing in 97 countries. This archive preserves IDG's landmark publications for researchers, technologists, and historians.

Source materials are drawn from the Internet Archive IDG collection, the Wayback Machine, and the McGovern family private archive. Curated by the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation.
